01

Say what you want. Together you turn it into a goal.

You don't need to be a programmer. Describe what you want in your own words; the agent asks what it needs to know and proposes a goal. One click puts it in the queue.

  • Testers' GitHub issues can be discussed the same way
  • Chatting never changes your code

02

One AI builds. The other checks. Then it looks at your app.

Every task is reviewed by the other agent before it's committed. After that Coodoo starts your app and looks at it, like a person would. Broken or unsafe? It goes back to the developer first.

  • Security problems are fixed before anything else
  • Lessons from rejected work are remembered next to the code

The agents work on their own. Within hard limits.

These rules are always on. No goal, agent or button can switch them off; Coodoo stops whatever tries.

✓Your repository stays. Never deleted, archived, transferred or made public.
✓History is never rewritten. No force pushes, no deleted branches or tags.
✓Keys stay secret. Encrypted, never shown, never committed.
✓The agents can't reach your login. They only write inside your project folder.
✓Commits in your name. Never in the AI's.
✓Work follows your flow. No step is ever skipped.

Frequently asked questions

Are Claude and ChatGPT included?

No. Coodoo is the team around the AI: the goals, the autopilot, the reviews, GitHub and the safety rules. The AI itself comes from your own subscription with Anthropic (Claude Pro or Max, for Claude Code) and/or OpenAI (ChatGPT Plus or Pro, for Codex). You take that out and pay for it yourself; it is not part of the Coodoo license.

Do I need to be able to program?

No. You describe what you want in plain words, and the AI team builds it. You don't need a terminal. It does help to look at what the team makes before you use it.

Do I need both Claude and ChatGPT?

One is enough. With both, one agent builds and the other reviews its work, which catches more mistakes.

Where does my code live?

On your own computer and in your own GitHub account. Coodoo runs on your computer; your code is not stored by CentreBrain. What the agents read is sent to the AI service you use (Anthropic or OpenAI), under their terms.

Which computers does Coodoo run on?

macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) and Windows (x64 and ARM).

Is it safe to let AI work on its own?

Coodoo has safety rules that are always on, such as never deleting your repository or rewriting history, and keeping your keys secret. They limit the risks but cannot rule them out: AI can make mistakes. Read the disclaimer and the risks of AI.
