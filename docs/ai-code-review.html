How it works

  1. 1The developer agent builds the task and writes tests.
  2. 2Coodoo runs the tests and starts your app to look at it.
  3. 3The reviewer reads the changes and the app report.
  4. 4Approved: the task is committed in your name. Changes needed: the developer fixes them and the reviewer looks again.

With one subscription

One subscription is enough: then the same AI does the review, in a separate step with a fresh look. Two different AIs catch more, which is why Coodoo works best with both.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need both Claude and ChatGPT?

No, one is enough. With both, one builds and the other reviews, which catches more mistakes.

Can I switch the review off?

Yes. Every phase can be switched on or off per project. We recommend keeping the review on.

Does the AI review replace my own check?

No. It catches a lot, but AI can make mistakes. Look at and try what the team made before you use it.
