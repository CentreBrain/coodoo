What is never allowed

These rules protect your code, your accounts and your keys, also when an agent makes a mistake or misunderstands a goal.

  • Your repository is never deleted, archived, transferred or made public
  • No force pushes, no rewritten history, no deleted branches or tags
  • Keys live encrypted in a vault, never in a commit, and show as REDACTED in every log
  • The agents write only in your project folder and cannot reach your GitHub login
  • Only pre-approved actions on GitHub, and commits in your name
  • Work reaches your main branch only through the steps of your flow

How it works

  1. 1An agent or command tries something a rule forbids.
  2. 2Coodoo stops it before anything changes.
  3. 3The goal stops or skips that step, and you get a notification.
  4. 4Everything a failsafe stopped is kept in a log you can read in Settings.

Limits, not a guarantee

The failsafes make working with AI a lot safer, but they cannot rule out every risk: AI can make mistakes, and the app the team builds runs with your own rights. Read the disclaimer and the risks of AI.

Frequently asked questions

Can I switch the failsafes off?

No. They are always on, also for you. That way a mistake can never undo them.

Can the AI see my passwords and keys?

Keys go into Coodoo’s encrypted vault and are blanked out in logs. The agents get no GitHub login; your app gets a key only when you hand it over.

Where can I see what was stopped?

In Settings → Failsafes, with what was tried, when and why it was stopped.
