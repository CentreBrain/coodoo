Disclaimer and the risks of AI

Last updated: 4 November 2026

In short

  • Coodoo lets AI write and run software on your computer. AI can make mistakes, also when it sounds sure of itself.
  • Check what the team makes before you use it, publish it or give it to others. You remain responsible for your project.
  • Claude and ChatGPT are not included. You use your own subscription with Anthropic and/or OpenAI, under their terms.
  • Coodoo’s safety rules limit the risks, but offer no guarantee. You use Coodoo at your own risk.

What Coodoo is, and what it is not

Coodoo is software by CentreBrain that runs on your own computer and organises AI coding agents as a team: it turns your idea into goals and tasks, lets the agents build, test and review, and puts the result on GitHub. The AI itself is not part of Coodoo. It comes from third-party services: Claude Code from Anthropic and Codex from OpenAI. You take out and pay for those services yourself; their own terms and privacy policies apply to them.

The risks of AI

Mistakes and made-up answers

AI models predict text; they do not understand your project the way a person does. Code can fail to work, work only in some cases, or do something other than you asked. An explanation can be wrong or simply invented, while sounding convincing.

Security holes

AI can write code with weaknesses: a login that can be bypassed, data that is visible to the wrong people, or a package with a known vulnerability. Coodoo checks for common problems, but no check finds everything. Be extra careful with apps that handle accounts, payments or personal data.

Losing work or data

The agents change files in your project, and the app they build can change data, for example in a database. Coodoo keeps your work in Git and on GitHub and never rewrites history, but a mistake can still remove or damage something. Keep backups of anything you cannot lose, and never let the team work on real production data.

AI that works on its own

With the autopilot, the agents run commands on your computer without asking you each time: installing packages, building, testing and starting your app. Coodoo limits what they can do (they write only in your project folder, cannot reach your GitHub login, and some actions are always refused), but these limits cannot rule out every risk. The app the team builds runs with your own rights when Coodoo starts it.

Privacy and confidential information

What the agents read, such as your code, your messages and screenshots, is sent to the AI service you use (Anthropic or OpenAI) and processed under their terms. Do not put other people’s personal data, passwords or confidential business information in a chat or a project unless you are allowed to share it with those services. Coodoo keeps keys from its vault out of commits and logs, but cannot see everything you type.

Costs

AI work uses your subscription’s limits or, with an API key, money per use. The costs Coodoo shows are estimates. Keep an eye on your usage with Anthropic and OpenAI themselves.

Copyright and licenses

Code written by AI can resemble existing code, and the packages it uses come with their own licenses. Check that you may use what is in your project the way you intend, especially before you sell or publish it.

Laws and rules for your app

If your app processes personal data, takes payments or uses AI itself, rules may apply to you, such as the GDPR (AVG) in Europe. Coodoo cannot judge whether your app meets them. That remains your responsibility.

Depending on other services

Coodoo depends on services of others, such as Anthropic, OpenAI and GitHub. They can change, become unavailable or change their prices and limits. CentreBrain has no control over that.

Critical applications

Do not use software the team made for medical, financial, legal or safety-critical purposes without a thorough check by a qualified professional.

What Coodoo does to limit the risks

  • Safety rules that are always on: your repository is never deleted or made public, history is never rewritten, and keys stay secret.
  • The agents write only in your project folder and cannot reach your GitHub login.
  • With two agents, one builds and the other reviews; Coodoo also starts your app and checks it, and scans for known security problems.
  • Work only reaches your main branch through the steps of your flow.

More on the safety rules and in the manual.

What you do yourself

  • Look at and try what the team made before you use or publish it.
  • Keep backups, and work with test data, not real customer data.
  • Put keys and passwords in Coodoo’s vault, never in a chat or a file.
  • Ask an expert when your app handles money, health or personal data.
