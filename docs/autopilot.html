The autopilot: from idea to working app.

Coodoo turns your idea into goals and tasks and lets an AI team work through them on its own: plan, build, test, review and deliver to GitHub. You watch, pause or step in whenever you like.

Download Coodoo See the price

A team that keeps going without you

Every goal goes through the same phases as in a real software team: research, design, planning, development, review and delivery. You choose which phases are on and which agent takes which role. With “Keep working” on, the team even thinks up sensible next goals itself.

  • Phases you switch on and off yourself
  • Pause and continue at any moment; nothing is lost
  • Every step in the run log, in plain words
  • A yellow card when it needs you, such as for a command only you may run

How it works

  1. 1Describe your idea in the chat, in your own words.
  2. 2Agree on a goal: the agent asks what it needs to know and proposes one.
  3. 3Start the autopilot. The team makes a plan and splits it into tasks.
  4. 4Task by task, one agent builds and the tests run.
  5. 5The other agent reviews, and Coodoo starts your app to see whether it works.
  6. 6The finished goal lands on your development branch on GitHub.

Good to know

The autopilot runs on your own computer, with your own Claude and/or ChatGPT subscription. While it works, Coodoo keeps your computer awake and shows how much of your subscription you use. Its safety rules are always on.

Frequently asked questions

Can I stop the autopilot at any time?

Yes. Pause it and continue later, or stop it. The work so far stays on its branch.

Does it keep working while I’m away?

Yes, as long as your computer is on and Coodoo is open. It keeps the computer from going to sleep while it works.

What if a task fails?

The run log says what went wrong. The team tries to fix it; when it needs you, a yellow card tells you what to do.
